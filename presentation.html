CORE ARCHITECTURE
Four Pillars of Pre-Publication Brand Governance
Pillar 01
Synchronous Gate

Multi-modal checks before publish: Evaluates headlines, body copy, disclaimers, hex colors, and embedded banner text simultaneously in <1.5s.

Pillar 02
Explainable Citations

Every flag maps to a rule: Zero black-box rejections. Cites exact Guideline ID, chapter title, specific rationale, and verified evidence URL.

Pillar 03
1-Click Suggestions

Fixes preserve tone and intent: Context-aware suggestions that eliminate legal risk without stripping the author's creative voice.

Pillar 04
Human Stage-Gates

People retain final authority: Strict role-based control plane (Creator Draft ➔ Engine Scan ➔ Manager Approval ➔ Signed Certificate).
